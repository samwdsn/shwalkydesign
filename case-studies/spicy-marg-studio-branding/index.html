A CREATIVE STUDIO WITH AN EXTRA KICK.

Spicy Marg Studio needed an identity that balanced creative credibility with the playful character behind its name. In a market where agencies often look and sound alike, the brief was to create a confident, distinctive brand that could communicate a broad creative offer without becoming generic.

WHERE HEAT MEETS SWEET.

I developed the complete brand direction, including the logo, colour palette, typography, imagery and tone of voice. Brand guidelines brought these elements together, while initial social graphics and website applications explored how the identity could retain its irreverent personality across different channels and formats.
