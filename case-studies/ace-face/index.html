MAKING GEN-Z SKINCARE FEEL REAL.

Ace Face needed to turn its existing identity into a convincing vision for a Gen Z skincare range. With limited physical products available, the brand required a prospectus that could communicate its audience, proposed range and creative direction to investors and manufacturing partners before production.

MAKING THE VISION CLEAR.

I developed the prospectus using the existing branding, creating product concepts, social assets and 3D packaging mockups. Together, these materials showed how the identity could work across a coherent range, giving stakeholders a clearer understanding of the proposed products and their position within the beauty market.
