TRADE JOURNALING MADE SMARTER WITH AI.

FX Notes needed a website to introduce its AI-assisted trading journal and encourage people to get started. The design pairs a direct product message with a light, spacious presentation, using soft colour and restrained interface details to make the software feel approachable rather than visually overwhelming.

MAKING AI FEEL MORE FAMILIAR.

I designed the website and supporting UI kit around pastel gradients, clean typography and a familiar design language associated with leading companies within the AI space. The experience introduces users to the software before they enter the platform, using recognisable interface patterns and clear calls to action to build confidence and guide visitors towards signing up.
