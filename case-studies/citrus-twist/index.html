A FRESH TWIST FOR SPARKLING DRINKS.

Citrus Twist was an exploration of how a canned sparkling juice could express freshness and flavour through a bold identity. The concept needed to feel colourful and energetic while remaining recognisable in a crowded drinks category, with a visual direction that could work beyond the can.

CONCEPTS BEYOND THE CAN.

I developed the identity and packaging design, then explored their wider application through 3D product renders, campaign graphics and social assets. These applications tested how the concept could hold together across different formats, connecting the product presentation with a broader, expressive brand world.
