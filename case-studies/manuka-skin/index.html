NATURAL INGREDIENTS AT THE HEART OF THE IDENTITY.

Manuka Skin needed an identity and packaging direction for skincare built around Manuka honey. The brief brought together natural ingredients, sensitive skincare and environmental awareness. The challenge was to communicate those qualities clearly while creating a distinctive presence within a crowded beauty category.

FROM NATURE TO FINISHED PRODUCT.

I created the logo, visual direction and packaging system, supported by a pattern inspired by the Manuka flower and honey bee. Applied across the product, retail box and brand materials, the identity combined expressive detail with simple packaging presentation that reflected the brand’s environmental considerations.
