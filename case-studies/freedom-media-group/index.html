BRINGING OUT-OF-HOME INTO THE DIGITAL AGE.

Freedom Media Group needed a modern website reflecting its expertise in out-of-home advertising and digital marketing. The challenge was to move beyond traditional perceptions of billboard advertising, creating an engaging digital experience that showcased campaigns, communicated services and encouraged enquiries while retaining the established brand identity.

A FRESH PERSPECTIVE ON A FAMILIAR IDENTITY.

I designed and prototyped the full website around Freedom’s signature purple and existing logo. Gradients, lighting effects and bespoke animated vector graphics brought energy to the experience, balancing expressive visuals with clear navigation, purposeful content and opportunities for lead generation.
