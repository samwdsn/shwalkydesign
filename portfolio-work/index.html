A hot identity with a cult following.

Branding // Packaging // Food & Drink

Sizzle Society needed an identity that captured the intensity of its hot sauce and the unconventional personality behind its name. The brief was to introduce its flagship blend with a distinctive visual presence, balancing bold expression with clear product communication and room for future flavours.

Bringing the signature heat.

I created the logo suite, iconography and wider art direction, then applied the identity to a custom bottle and label design with embossed detailing. A series of 3D product renders brought the packaging to life and explored how the brand could extend into future campaigns.

Finding the chemistry behind the industry.

Branding // Art Direction // Agency

Chemistry + Industry needed a contemporary identity for its approach to operations management. The challenge was to make a complex service feel approachable and credible without relying on familiar corporate styling. The brand needed to speak to growing businesses while expressing the thinking behind its offer.

A bonded visual language.

I developed the identity around the idea of “the chemistry behind the industry”, using molecular forms to connect the logo suite, iconography and supporting graphics. Brand guidelines established a consistent direction across social content, printed collateral and the website, giving the business a recognisable visual foundation.

Natural ingredients at the heart of the identity.

Branding // Packaging // Skincare

Manuka Skin needed an identity and packaging direction for skincare built around Manuka honey. The brief brought together natural ingredients, sensitive skincare and environmental awareness. The challenge was to communicate those qualities clearly while creating a distinctive presence within a crowded beauty category.

From nature to finished product.

I created the logo, visual direction and packaging system, supported by a pattern inspired by the Manuka flower and honey bee. Applied across the product, retail box and brand materials, the identity combined expressive detail with simple packaging presentation that reflected the brand’s environmental considerations.

Making Gen-Z skincare feel real.

Branding // Packaging // Beauty

Ace Face needed to turn its existing identity into a convincing vision for a Gen Z skincare range. With limited physical products available, the brand required a prospectus that could communicate its audience, proposed range and creative direction to investors and manufacturing partners before production.

Making the vision clear.

I developed the prospectus using the existing branding, creating product concepts, social assets and 3D packaging mockups. Together, these materials showed how the identity could work across a coherent range, giving stakeholders a clearer understanding of the proposed products and their position within the beauty market.
